# Lund GTFS Sverige 2 fixed-source fixture

Five actual trip records and their complete stop-time chains are selected from the official 2025-10-04 historical archive. Related agency, stop, route, calendar, calendar_dates and feed_info rows are retained. Rows are reordered deterministically and CSV is serialized as UTF-8/LF; trip times, identifiers, coordinates and service dates are unchanged. This is a tiny contract fixture, not a complete timetable.

Source: https://data.samtrafiken.se/trafiklab/gtfs-sverige-2/2025/10/sweden-20251004.zip
