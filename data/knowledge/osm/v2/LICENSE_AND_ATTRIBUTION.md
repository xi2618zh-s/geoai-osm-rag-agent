# OSM knowledge data license and attribution

This notice applies only to the text knowledge data under `data/knowledge/osm/v2/` and derived M03 knowledge artifacts. It does not select or change the license of the repository's software code.

The source text is from the OpenStreetMap Wiki and is used under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0 (CC BY-SA 2.0). The official policy page is frozen at [oldid 3006732](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/w/index.php?title=Wiki_content_license&oldid=3006732). That policy states that Wiki text is CC BY-SA 2.0 by default, identifies page-level exceptions, and separately cautions that media may use other licenses.

M03 therefore includes text only. It does not fetch or redistribute images, screenshots, maps, or other media. A page that declares separate licensing is rejected from automatic ingestion pending manual compatibility review.

Each raw snapshot manifest attributes its fixed page revision to OpenStreetMap Wiki contributors, records the page and history URLs, identifies CC BY-SA 2.0, and describes extraction changes. Project-authored or Codex-assisted Chinese names, concise summaries, aliases, and hard-negative explanations are marked as project curation and accompany the knowledge data under CC BY-SA 2.0.
